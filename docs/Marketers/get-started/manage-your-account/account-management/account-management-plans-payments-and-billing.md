<div id="plan-usage-and-units" />

<Accordion title="Usage, units, and premium features">

Payment for your plan is calculated according to your **plan usage** and **premium features**.

#### Plan usage

Each plan is based on measurement of a specific [unit](/docs/account-management-plans-payments-and-billing#plan-units). Unit usage is calculated monthly or annually. The number of units used during the plan cycle (month or year) is deducted from the total package units.

> 📘 **Note**
>
> You can [download usage reports](/docs/account-management-plans-payments-and-billing#download-a-usage-report) to get detailed information about your unit usage.

<span id="zd-premium-features"></span>**Exceeding plan usage**

When exceeding the number of units in your plan cycle, extra costs are applied. You can see all costs in the [**Plan details**](/docs/account-management-plans-payments-and-billing#details) section. To help prevent exceeding plan usage or reduce extra costs, alerts are sent via the notification center in the following cases:

* Your plan units are being rapidly used
* You're getting close to reaching your allocated units (75% of your plan units)
* You've exceeded the usage of your plan units

You can check with your CSM or contact [hello@appsflyer.com](mailto:hello@appsflyer.com) for upgrade options.

#### Premium features

Any [add-ons](/docs/account-management-plans-payments-and-billing#premium-add-on-units) you've included in your plan are premium features. For example:

* <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/protect360-anti-fraud-guide">Protect360</Anchor> (mobile fraud protection)

To learn more about these add-ons and their potential benefits for your business, check with your CSM or contact [hello@appsflyer.com](mailto:hello@appsflyer.com).

#### ROI360 dollar units

The ROI360 add-on measures the return on investments of your ad marketing. The add-on package includes a set number of recorded dollar consumption units for ad cost, in-app ad revenue, and in-app purchase revenue (including subscriptions).

* Each unit represents $1 of the recorded ROI360 measurement
* Recorded dollars are presented in USD currency
* When exceeding the package units, extra costs are applied
* Recorded ROI360 data includes:
  * Cost data from up to 4 days after the initial cost day
  * In-app ads data from up to 3 days after the initial revenue day
* Recorded ROI360 data doesn't include:
  * In-app purchases or subscription refunds
  * Invalid in-app purchases or subscriptions

<div id="understand-billing-for-extended-platform-apps" />

<Accordion title="Understand billing for extended platform apps">

Extended platform apps include apps running on CTV, PC, console, and other platforms that aren’t iOS, Android, Windows phone, PBA, or website.

Starting February 1, 2026, non-organic installs from extended platform apps (for example, CTV, PC, and console) are included in billable conversions, according to your existing conversion-based commercial terms.

#### What is billed

* Non-organic installs.
* Non-organic re-engagements.
* Owned media activity.

#### What isn’t billed

* Organic installs and re-engagements.

For accounts on a conversions package, extended platform conversions are billed at the same unit cost as mobile conversions.

If you use Adaptive Pricing, extended platform activity can increase credit consumption for billed installs and related usage.

MAU-based billing isn’t supported for extended platform apps.

</Accordion>

### Usage Report

The Usage Report page shows your billable conversions and your return on investments—for ROI360 subscribers. From this page, you can:

* Filter data by dates, apps, media sources, event sources, and event types.
* [Download](#download-a-usage-report) a report according to the selected filters.

#### Access to the Usage Report page

**Prerequisite:**

* Only account admins and account users with the following permissions can access the Usage Report page:
  * Access the **Account** page
  * Access to all apps, media sources, and geos

**To access the Usage Report page:**

* From the [**Account**](#account-page) page > **My Plan**, click **Usage report** on the top-right.

> 📘 **Note**
>
> Usage report isn't supported for the Monthly Active User (MAU) plan.

#### Data included in the usage report

* The data shown in the report may be **complete or partial,** depending on the current day of the month and the selected date range:

| Complete/Partial | Current day of the month | Selected date range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span id="complete"></span>Complete | 10th and onwards | Previous month |
| <span id="partial"></span>Partial | Between 1st-9th | Previous month |
| <span id="partial::64822c61"></span>Partial | Any day of the month | Current month |

* All data in the report is aggregated and includes billable conversions and ROI360 events. Non-chargeable events, such as organic installs and fraud, aren’t included.
* based on the selected filters.
* By default, the date range shows data from the 1st of the current month to the present day. You can change this by selecting different dates from the filter at the top of the table.
* Data in the report is retained for 24 months.

> 📘 **Note**
>
> * Usage reports and invoices are calculated based on the UTC+0 time zone. As a result, there may be slight discrepancies between usage reports and the data reported on dashboards, Cohort reports, and other aggregated reports.
> * Data in the usage report remains **non-transparent** whether or not <Anchor target="_blank" href="/partners/docs/getting-started-agency-onboarding#transparency">**Allow transparency**</Anchor> is turned on, meaning they show the agency name and not the attributed media source

### **Payment types**

##### **Hands-free recurring payments**

Save your preferred payment method once, and we’ll automatically charge it when an invoice is due. This is the **most convenient** way to ensure continuous service.

To Set Up, go to **Payments & invoices** → **Update Payment Method**.

**For US customers,** **ACH** provides the most reliable recurring payment experience.

> 📘 **Note**
>
> Automatic charges will not be processed if you have a prepayment plan, a credit balance, the invoice was paid by wire transfer, or there is a billing issue.

##### **PayNow (manual invoice payment)**

Pay open invoices manually at any time directly through the portal.

To use, go to **Payments & invoices**, select the open invoice, and click **Pay**.

**For US Customers,** **ACH PayNow** offers the fastest and most consistent manual payment experience.

Regardless of your regular payment method, you can repay overdue invoices using your **preferred payment method** through a [**one-time Pay Now payment**](/docs/account-management-plans-payments-and-billing#set-up). The payment method you use will **not** be saved.

Paying overdue invoices for accounts with a [status](/docs/account-management-plans-payments-and-billing#account-status) of **Pending suspension** or **Suspended** will return the account to **Active**.

### Conversion measurement options for iOS app billing

Since the introduction of iOS 14.5, AppsFlyer offers two possible methods to determine campaign performance and attribution for iOS apps:

* <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/single-source-of-truth-ssot-guide-for-ios-attribution">**Single Source of Truth (SSOT)**</Anchor>: If you have SSOT configured in AppsFlyer SKAN Conversion Studio, this method will automatically be used for billing.
* **If you haven't turned on SSOT:** AppsFlyer runs a side-by-side comparison of the number of daily conversions measured by SKAdNetwork and those measured by our privacy-centric RTA (real-time attribution) for each media source. These two methods run independently and in parallel. The higher of the two numbers is taken into account for billing. This ensures that you're never charged twice for a conversion event.
