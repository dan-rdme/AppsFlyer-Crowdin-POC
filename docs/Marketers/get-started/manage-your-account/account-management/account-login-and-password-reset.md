> **At a glance:** Log in to AppsFlyer, add multiple accounts, and reset user passwords.

## Log in instructions

Log in instructions for first-time and existing users. This includes a new account creation, a new user invitation, or how to log in as an existing user.

**Note**: For instructions on how to create a user see <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/user-management#creating-users">here</Anchor>.


<div id="logging-in-for-the-first-time" />

<Accordion title="Logging in for the first time">

Whether you just signed up or have just been invited to AppsFlyer, you will receive an email with an activation link.

To sign in to AppsFlyer:

1. Open the AppsFlyer activation email
2. **For the first user on an account**:
   1. Click the activation link in the email body  
      **Note**: The user created when the account is opened is the "account owner" and is assigned the <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/user-management#account-owner">Admin role by default.</Anchor>
3. **For users using an invite**:
   1. Copy the verification code in the email body
   2. Click the **AppsFlyer login page** button in the email body
   3. Enter your code  
      **Note:**  Verification codes are valid for 15 minutes. If the verification code expires, click **Resend Code** to receive a new one
4. Create a password

</Accordion>

<div id="existing-users" />

<Accordion title="Existing users">

Existing users can log into AppsFlyer through the <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://hq1.appsflyer.com/auth/login">main login page</Anchor>.

Log in to AppsFlyer can be done using your:

* Credentials (username and password)
* Google login
* <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/sso-authentication">Single Sign-on</Anchor>

**Note**: If your administrator configured a <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/implementing-and-using-2fa-login">two-factor authentication (2FA)</Anchor> for your account, you'll be asked to verify the login using another authentication method.

</Accordion>

<div id="twofactor-authentication-2fa" />

<Accordion title="Two-factor authentication (2FA)">

If 2FA is enabled in the Security Management settings, it will be activated after your initial login.

[To reset a user's 2FA, see here](/docs/account-login-and-password-reset#resetting-twofactor-authentication-2fa).

</Accordion>

## Password, Login, or 2FA reset

Password reset for existing users and users who did not complete the initial registration or invitation process.

<div id="existing-users-and-invited-users-who-have-not-activated-yet" />

<Accordion title="Existing users and invited users who have not activated yet">

To reset your password:

1. On the AppsFlyer <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://hq1.appsflyer.com/auth/login">main login page</Anchor>, click **Forgot password?**
2. A one-time verification code will be sent to your email
3. Copy the verification code in the email body
4. Click the **AppsFlyer login page** button in the email body
5. Enter your code  
   **Note:**  Verification codes are valid for 15 minutes  
   If the verification code expires, click **Resend Code** to receive a new one
6. Create a new password

</Accordion>

<div id="users-who-have-not-completed-the-initial-registration" />

<Accordion title="Users who have not completed the initial registration">

When signing up to AppsFlyer, an email invitation was sent to your email. The activation link in the email is valid for 7 days. To complete sign up, do the following:

1. Open the email
2. Click the activation **AppsFlyer sign up page** button the email body  
   **Note**: The user created at the time the account was opened, the "account owner", is assigned the <Anchor target="_blank" href="/marketers/docs/user-management#account-owner">Admin role by default.</Anchor>
   1. If the link has expired, complete the sign up  from the beginning
3. Create a password

</Accordion>

<div id="resetting-twofactor-authentication-2fa" />

<Accordion title="Reset a user's two-factor authentication (2FA)">

If your account uses 2FA, there may be occasions on which you need to reset a user’s 2FA settings. Do so as follows:

1. On the **Users** tab, click the **Actions** button <Image alt="actions_icon.png" src="https://files.readme.io/88a240d0697e042024a793d26bb97141a66079963636908761c8feb9db2ef4f4-33118830755985.png" width="25px" /> on the far-right side of the user’s row.
2. Select **Reset 2FA** from the menu.

**Note:** An admin can reset any other user's 2FA other than their own. To reset the account owner's 2FA, contact AppsFlyer Support team.

</Accordion>
